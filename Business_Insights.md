# Business Insights & Recommendations

## 1. Sales Performance
Electronics is the leading revenue-generating category in the analyzed dataset.

**Recommendation:** Monitor electronics inventory, supplier performance and product availability.

## 2. Delivery Performance
Delivery status analysis indicates a considerable proportion of delayed transactions.

**Recommendation:** Investigate logistics bottlenecks, dispatch delays and shipping operations.

## 3. Customer Payment Preferences
Transactions are distributed across multiple payment methods.

**Recommendation:** Maintain multiple payment options and monitor payment preferences.

## 4. Product Returns
Category-level return rate analysis helps identify segments that require additional investigation.

**Recommendation:** Analyze return reasons, customer reviews and product quality issues.

## Conclusion

The dashboard provides an integrated view of sales, products, customers and delivery operations. It demonstrates how Power BI, Power Query and DAX can transform transaction-level data into interactive business intelligence reports.
